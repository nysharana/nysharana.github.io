Course 03 · Processhow it gets cooked

From Brief to Table

Every project is a sit-down dinner. Keep scrolling and the ball will take you course by course.

  1. Step 01 · Research

    Who’s coming to dinner?

    Who’s at the table, what they’re hungry for, what they can’t stand. I keep asking until I know the why before I touch the how.

    the rabbit hole, frequently visited.
  2. Step 02 · Experiment

    Small plates first.

    Lots of little ideas, passed around the table. Try every direction, keep what’s interesting, clear away what isn’t.

    a little bit of whimsy.
  3. Step 03 · Find the fit

    The crowd favourite.

    One idea fits the brief so well the whole table leans in. That’s the one we build the meal around.

    you just know.
  4. Step 04 · Iterate

    Second helpings.

    Push it, polish it, pass it round for opinions and go again until it’s right. Ideas get challenged here, on purpose.

    and again. and again.
  5. Step 05 · Deliver

    Served on time. Always.

    On the table when I said it would be, with nothing left half-done. If I say I’ll get something done, consider it done.

    deadlines: non-negotiable.

calm, composed, quietly obsessive.
